A little about us · 1/4
🌱

Good food can do more than fill a plate.

ShareBite is a campus-focused concept built around a simple idea: make food easier to access, help reduce avoidable food waste, and create small earning opportunities for students.

Our story starts with a question: what if extra food could become an opportunity instead of going to waste? We want to connect food sharers, students looking for affordable meals, and campus delivery helpers in one convenient place.
